# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first release: a native IPTV player for Xtream Codes providers, for
Linux and macOS.

- Several providers at once, each with a name of your choosing; one
  provider's groups or all of them, every row tagged with its provider.
- Home: the channel watched last, still playing; then what was left
  unfinished, favorite channels on now, and the newest movies.
- Live TV: groups, logos, what is on now and next, a preview, fast zapping.
- Favorites across providers, in your order: channels, movies and series,
  from `F` or a heart anywhere, the player included (`H`).
- The guide: a grid from each provider's XMLTV, kept fresh in the
  background, with catch-up for ended programmes.
- Movies and series: poster grids, a page per title, seasons and
  episodes, resume and the next episode.
- Search at the top of every screen, with results as you type.
- The player: pause and rewind within live, Go live, seek, audio and
  subtitle menus, stream details, fullscreen.
- Settings: refresh intervals, a guide time shift, decoding, preferred
  languages, the live stream format, the sidebar, the start screen, the
  clock, storage and Clear cache; About names the version and commit.
- Release builds: Flatpak and AppImage for x86_64 and arm64, and a
  universal macOS app.
